# stahpwatch roadmap

What's planned for stahpwatch, roughly in order. [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) describes the app as it is today.

## Planned

- **Tap the dial to start, pause and resume,** with a long-press to stop (only while paused).
- **Optionally hide the buttons,** building on tap to start.
- **Swipe to Customize,** alongside the Customize button.
- **Dial position presets,** such as top, center or bottom.
- **Milestone flash:** flash the screen at a chosen interval, such as every 90 seconds. Needs design first, including what happens when the screen is locked.
- **Laps:** optional, and off by default.
- **Clean up Looks:** scope to be decided. It may include separate text sizing, such as for the hundredths.
- **Privacy policy and privacy manifest,** needed for the App Store.
- **The owner's own app icon,** replacing the Instrument placeholder.

## Later

- **App Store release.**
- **Android version,** following the port notes in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md#android-port-notes).
